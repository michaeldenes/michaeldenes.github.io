# Hobbies and Fun

Cycling is one of my favourite things. If I'm not at work, I'm probably out cycling.

In 2025, I "competed" in my first ultra-endurance cycling event, the **Utrecht Ultra**. I've ridden plenty of 100+ and 200+km rides, but never a 1000km ride in one go! I didn't quite get the nutrition right (eat more!) nor the sleep (weather forecasts were overly optimistic for the overnight temperatures).

I placed 77th overall, but I think with the right strategy, a top 50 is quite possible. With all that I learnt in 2025, I've signed up again for the 2026 edition through Belgium! Stay tuned.


**Update 2026**

I competed again in the **Utrecht Ultra** in 2026. This year was a new beast, with temperatures rising to over 35℃ in parts. On a new parcours, I couldn't quite handle the heat, and ended up in 85th place. Managed the sleep a lot better this year, but struggled in the heat wave! At least I cycled through some really stunning parts of Belgium and the Netherlands.
